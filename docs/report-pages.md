# Report Pages

## Summary
Executive overview of retail performance using revenue, gross margin, product-line, geography, and time-based analysis.

## Top Revenue
Focuses on the strongest-performing products by revenue and year-over-year revenue performance.

## Bottom Revenue
Highlights underperforming products using revenue and year-over-year comparisons.

## Achievement
Compares actual revenue performance with planned revenue and tracks achievement.

## Top Margin
Analyzes the strongest gross-margin performance by product line and related dimensions.

## Bottom Margin
Highlights lower-margin performance and supports margin-focused diagnostics.

## Demand
Analyzes quantity sold and product demand patterns over time.

## What If
Uses a Price Increase parameter to model the effect of pricing changes on revenue and profit.

## Tooltip Details
Supporting tooltip page used to provide contextual detail within the report.
